# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AgentSec is a security project, and its community should be safe, respectful,
and technically rigorous. Participants are expected to communicate in good
faith, welcome constructive disagreement, protect private information, and keep
security research within authorized and lawful boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
deliberate disclosure of private material, credential abuse, destructive
testing, or instructions intended to enable unauthorized compromise.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the security contact described in
[`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove comments, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the project
and its community.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant community standard.
